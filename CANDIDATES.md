# Candidates — The Funniest Out-of-Office (make.fun)

Mission: one line of out-of-office auto-reply, funniest possible, ≤ 140 characters.
Judging: pairwise, blind, judge model picks the funnier of two lines (margin 1–5),
3 Swiss rounds + playoff among top 10. No leaderboard is visible — no past
winners or live ranks to study (noted in JUDGING-CHECKLIST.md).

Scoring rubric (25 points total):
- Funniness (10) — does it actually make the reader laugh?
- OOO auto-reply shape (4) — reads as a genuine out-of-office reply
- Instant landing (4) — the joke lands on first read, no setup required
- Originality (4) — not a stock OOO cliché
- Surprise twist (3) — an unexpected turn at the end

C6a wins: "All decisions have been delegated" is more formal/absurd than "I've
delegated", which sharpens the contrast with the punchline "it has chosen chaos".
C10 is strong but "ignore you" is a weaker, more expected payoff than "chaos".
